About alignhsc

What is alignhsc?

alignhsc is a free, student-built platform designed to make HSC preparation easier. From finding topic-specific questions to creating custom practice papers, the goal is simple: spend less time searching and more time studying.

Starting with Mathematics Advanced, alignhsc aims to expand across HSC subjects, bringing practice questions and study tools together in one place.

How did it come to be?

As someone who's survived the HSC, I know the struggle.

You sit down, fully prepared to lock in, open approximately 17 different PDFs, spend 40 minutes looking for a question on one specific topic, and somehow manage to do absolutely zero studying.

So I decided to do something about it.

And that's how alignhsc came to be → the study resource I wish I'd had during my HSC.
